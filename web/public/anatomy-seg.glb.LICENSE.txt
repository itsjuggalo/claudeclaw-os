anatomy-seg.glb — FULL-BODY segmented human anatomy (skeleton + muscles + vessels
+ nerves), ~2254 separately named meshes, meshopt-compressed and quantized, with
textures/UVs stripped (the viewer recolors by tissue at runtime). Derived from
BodyParts3D (Database Center for Life Science) via the Z-Anatomy / AnatomyTOOL
Open3DModel lineage.
