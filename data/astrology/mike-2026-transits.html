Mike E · Scorpio Sun · Pisces Moon · Cancer Rising · Month by month, January–December

The Shape of Your Year

2026 has one dominant storyline for you: Jupiter — the planet of expansion — spends the first half of the year finishing a 12-year cycle in your hidden 12th house, then crosses your Ascendant in June and begins a brand-new 12-year chapter of your life. First-half 2026 is the cocoon: closing loops, quiet building, releasing what's expired. From June onward you step into visibility, vitality, and personal luck that keeps building into 2027.

Around that spine: the once-in-36-years Saturn–Neptune conjunction in February lights up your career sector and asks you to give your long-term vision a real structure; Uranus enters Gemini in April and starts rewiring your networks and tech stack for the next seven years; and August's total solar eclipse lands exactly on your natal Chiron in the 2nd house — the single most personal transit of your year, aimed straight at the money-and-self-worth wound. Themes, not fate: these are the winds; you still steer the boat.

January Quiet Fire Jupiter retrograde re-crossing your natal Mars · Mars season in your opposite sign

The year opens with Jupiter sliding backward over your natal Mars — a re-do pass on something you fired up in late 2025. A project, plan, or bold move from the fall comes back for revision, and the second draft is better. Energy runs high but inward: you'll want to work, but behind closed doors. With transiting planets moving through your 7th house early in the month, partnerships and one-on-one dealings ask for patience — people may feel pushier than usual; that's the mirror, not the enemy.

Best move: revise, don't launch. Rework the fall's big idea in private — June is when it goes public.

February The Vision Gets a Skeleton Saturn–Neptune conjunction at 0° Aries (Feb 20) in your career sector · Solar eclipse Feb 17 in your 8th house

This is the rarest sky of the year. Saturn (structure) and Neptune (dream) fuse at the very start of Aries — in your 9th/10th-house career-and-beliefs zone. The dream you've been carrying about what your work could become stops being a fantasy and demands a blueprint: write it down, put dates on it, make it real or release it. Two days earlier, a solar eclipse in your 8th house of shared money and other people's resources opens a new chapter around debts, joint finances, or how outside money flows through your life — a seed planted now matures over six months.

Best move: spend one evening writing the 5-year vision as if it were a spec. Saturn will hold you to it — kindly.

March Green Light Jupiter stations direct (Mar 10) · Total lunar eclipse Mar 3 in your 3rd house

The lunar eclipse on the 3rd lights up your communication zone — a conversation, message, or piece of information reaches a dramatic conclusion; something you've needed to say (or hear) finally lands. A week later Jupiter turns direct after four months retrograde, and the whole first-quarter molasses feeling starts to lift. Projects stalled since November begin moving on their own. Your Pisces Moon gets fed this month — intuition runs unusually loud and unusually accurate.

Best move: have the conversation you've been postponing. The eclipse clears the air for you.

April The Rewire Begins Uranus enters Gemini (Apr 26) — a 7-year era opens in your 11th house

Uranus changes signs only every seven years, and this month it enters Gemini and your 11th house of networks, communities, technology, and future plans. Between now and 2033, your circle gets electrified: old groups dissolve, strange and brilliant new allies appear, and technology becomes the connective tissue of your social and professional world. The people you'll consider essential in 2030? You may meet the first of them within months of this ingress. April itself feels restless — an itch to shake up who you spend time with and what tribe you belong to.

Best move: say yes to the odd invitation. Uranus delivers its gifts through the door you almost didn't open.

May Full Throttle Jupiter conjunct your natal Mars (23° Cancer) — the energy peak of the year

Jupiter makes its final, direct pass over your natal Mars — the planet of expansion supercharging the planet of drive, right on your Ascendant. This is the highest-octane month of your 2026: stamina, courage, appetite, and confidence all run hot. Whatever you push hard in May tends to break through. The only warning label: Jupiter magnifies Mars's shadow too — overcommitment, overtraining, over-risking. Big is fine; reckless is not. Your body is the instrument this month — it will do remarkable things if you also let it recover.

Best move: schedule your boldest physical and professional pushes for May — and cap the downside on every risk before you take it.

June The Door Opens Jupiter crosses your Ascendant (early June), enters Leo Jun 30 · Uranus opposes your natal Mercury

The headline transit of your year. Jupiter crosses your 26° Cancer Ascendant and a new 12-year cycle of growth begins — the last time this happened was mid-2014; think about what began in your life then. You become more visible, luckier in person, physically bigger-feeling (watch the appetite), and opportunities start finding you. Simultaneously Uranus opposes your natal Mercury: ideas arrive like lightning, plans flip fast, and a mental rut you've been in for years cracks open. Expect at least one genuinely surprising piece of news or insight that changes how you think about your work.

Best move: update everything public-facing — photos, profiles, the way you present yourself. The world is about to look at you more; give it the current version.

July Integration Jupiter fresh in Leo, your 1st house · Saturn stations retrograde (late July)

Jupiter settles into Leo and your 1st house — confidence with flair. After May's push and June's threshold, July asks you to integrate: the new chapter is open, now inhabit it. Saturn stationing retrograde in your career zone pulls one professional structure back onto the workbench — a plan from February needs a revision pass; don't read it as failure, it's quality control. Health and body respond dramatically well to attention this month; Jupiter in the 1st rewards anyone who trains, and punishes no one who rests.

Best move: pick the one February commitment that matters most and refine it. Depth over breadth until Saturn wraps its review in December.

August The Eclipse on the Wound Total solar eclipse Aug 12 at 20° Leo — exactly on your natal Chiron · Lunar eclipse Aug 28 conjunct your natal Moon

The most personal month of your year, by far. The total solar eclipse lands within two degrees of your natal Chiron in the 2nd house — the money-and-self-worth wound gets illuminated so it can finally heal. Something happens around income, value, or what you charge that touches the old nerve: am I worth it? The eclipse's answer is yes — but it may make you prove it to yourself first. Then the Aug 28 lunar eclipse lands near your natal Pisces Moon in the 8th: an emotional full-body release around intimacy, debts, or something long-buried. Eclipse months are lived forward and understood backward — don't force big decisions in the eclipse windows; let the dust settle two weeks.

Best move: raise your rates, claim your value, or renegotiate something financial — and journal what the two eclipses stir up. This is the year's deepest healing window.

September The Growth Check Jupiter in Leo squares your natal Scorpio Sun

Jupiter squares your Sun — a friction aspect, but the friendly kind: growth pressing against identity. You'll feel pulled to expand in a direction that your deeper Scorpio self isn't fully sold on — more visibility, more commitments, more spending, more yes. The square asks one question repeatedly: is this expansion, or just inflation? Opportunities this month are real but oversized as presented; take the meat, leave the garnish. Watch optimism in money decisions specifically — Jupiter squares make everything look like it can only go up.

Best move: before any big yes this month, cut the commitment in half and see if you still want it. If yes — proceed.

October Power Rebalanced Pluto stations direct in your 7th house · Jupiter approaches your 2nd house

Pluto — your chart ruler — turns direct in your partnership house, ending a five-month review of who holds power in your closest one-on-one bonds. A relationship dynamic (business or personal) that's been quietly renegotiating itself since spring now locks into its new form, and it's more honest than the old one. Meanwhile Jupiter crosses into your 2nd house of income: the visibility you gained since June starts converting into actual money. The faucet doesn't gush yet — but it audibly turns on.

Best move: formalize the partnership terms that shifted this year — say the quiet part out loud, put it in writing.

November The Healer's Payday Solar return Nov 3 · Jupiter conjunct your natal Chiron in the 2nd house

Happy birthday — and your solar-return year opens with Jupiter sitting exactly on natal Chiron, finishing what August's eclipse started. The money wound gets its balm: expect a concrete event — a payment, an opportunity, a client, a validation — that lands precisely where you used to feel undervalued. This is also the classic "wounded healer becomes paid healer" transit: your hardest-earned lessons become your most bankable skill. The birthday-year theme it sets: worth, owned. Late month, Jupiter's square to your natal Pluto adds ambition heat — wanting more is fine; obsessing is the tell to step back.

Best move: launch or price the offering built on what you had to heal in yourself. It's the year's most-blessed bet.

December Structure Locks In Saturn stations direct in your career sector · year-end consolidation

Saturn turns direct and the career blueprint from February — revised since July — finally gets its stamp. The path for 2027 becomes visibly concrete: 2027 brings Saturn to the very top of your chart, a career culmination that only comes every 29 years, and December 2026 is when you can first see its outline clearly. The year closes quieter than it ran: consolidate gains, close the books honestly, and rest the Pisces Moon properly before the climb. What you built in the 2026 cocoon-and-emergence is the launchpad; 2027 is the ascent.

Best move: write the year-end review — what the cocoon phase ended, what June opened, what the eclipses healed. Then plan 2027 like Saturn is watching. It is.
