Mike E · Aries Midheaven · Mars rising in Cancer · Uranus–Neptune in the 6th

The Career Signature

Your career story is written in three linked placements, and together they say one thing loudly: you were never built for employment — you were built for ownership.

Your Midheaven — the career point — is in Aries: the sign of the pioneer, the first-mover, the one who starts things. Aries MC people suffocate under bosses and org charts; they need to hunt their own game. The ruler of that Midheaven is Mars, sitting right on your Ascendant in Cancer — meaning your career and your personal identity are fused. Your work IS you: your name, your hands, your presence, your reputation. And Mars in Cancer aims all that drive at protective, nurturing, home-based enterprise — the warrior who fights by taking care of people.

Then the engine room: Uranus and Neptune conjunct in Capricorn in your 6th house of daily work. Uranus = technology, systems, automation, rebellion against the 9-to-5. Neptune = healing, intuition, dissolving other people's pain. Capricorn = build it into a real, disciplined business. One house, one conjunction, both careers: the healer and the engineer are the same job description in your chart. Massage therapy and building automated systems aren't a strange combination for you — they're the two hands of the same design.

How You're Built to Earn Chiron in Leo, 2nd house · 2nd ruled by the Sun in Scorpio

Your income house is ruled by your Scorpio Sun — money comes through depth, intensity, and mastery, not volume. You earn best when you go deeper than competitors, not broader: the specialist's premium, not the generalist's hourly. Scorpio-ruled income also favors recurring, compounding structures — retainers, memberships, subscriptions, positions that grow while you sleep — over one-off transactions.

Chiron in Leo sitting in that money house is the career wound and the career gift in one: a deep sensitivity about being valued, probably old underearning or under-charging pain — and the classic astrological signature of someone whose greatest earning power comes from what they had to heal in themselves. The practitioner who's felt pain gives the best treatment. The trader who's blown up trades the most carefully. Your scars are your résumé.

Earning rule from the chart: charge for depth and mastery, structure income to recur, and never price from the wound — price from the craft.

The Work Style Sun in the 4th · North Node in the 5th · Jupiter in the 3rd

Sun in the 4th house: your command center is home. You will always do your most powerful work from your own territory — the home office, the home studio, the sovereign base. Corporate settings drain you at the identity level; your own roof feeds you.

North Node in Sagittarius in the 5th: the destiny arrow points at creative, self-expressed, even speculative work — building things with your name on them, taking calculated risks, publishing, teaching what you know. Careers where you're the visible creator beat careers where you're the hidden operator, even though the hidden-operator role feels safer to your Scorpio side. The chart's dare: put your name on it.

Jupiter in Libra in the 3rd: your luck multiplies through communication — writing, content, messages, local word-of-mouth, one-on-one conversations that turn into clients and allies. Historically your best opportunities have probably arrived through someone talking about you. That's not chance; it's a placement. Feed it deliberately.

Partnership & the Long Game Saturn in Aquarius, 7th house

Saturn in your partnership house says your career matures through serious, contract-grade alliances — but slowly and selectively. Early collaborations tend to teach hard lessons; later ones (post-30, and you're there) become genuine force multipliers. The pattern to trust: few partners, deep terms, everything in writing. Aquarius flavor means your best allies are unconventional — technical people, remote people, people from odd corners of the internet rather than the local chamber of commerce.

Saturn here also promises the late-bloomer arc: your career is built like a compound-interest curve, not a lottery ticket. Unimpressive early, unstoppable late. The 30s are the inflection; the 40s and 50s are the harvest.
