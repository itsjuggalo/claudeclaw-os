There's also a quieter promise here: Saturn rules your 7th and your Uranus–Neptune 6th-house conjunction sits in Saturn's own sign, Capricorn. Translation: your best alliances will grow out of the craft itself — people who respect the work first and the person second, which for a Cancer-rising Scorpio Sun who guards the inner world is exactly the right order of operations.

Partnership rules: structure is a service — formalize the client experience fully. Few allies, deep terms, all in writing. Vet at Saturn speed; but when the technical ally appears mid-30s onward, don't reflexively refuse.
